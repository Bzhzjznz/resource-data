3.Damini (IITM Pune / Ministry of Earth Sciences).
What it does: Lightning and severe squall tracker.


Relevance for farmers: Lightning is one of the leading causes of fatal weather incidents in rural India. The app monitors thunderstorm and lightning strikes within a 20–40 km radius to give field workers immediate warnings to seek shelter.

4.mKisan Portal & SMS Advisory.
Format: Web portal & SMS push service.

What it does: Allows scientists and block extension officers to push localized agromet advisories directly to registered farmers via text/IVR based on meteorological updates.
